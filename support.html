Frequently asked

My takes sound late (or early).

Run the timing measurement: Audio setup → Measure this route · 30 s. Every route (speaker, wired headphones, each Bluetooth device, each USB interface) is measured separately and remembered. Measure again if you change headphones.

Can I record with AirPods?

Yes — playback goes to the AirPods, the iPhone's own mic records, and the take is shifted by the measured Bluetooth delay. Live monitoring of your own voice over Bluetooth is off because the delay is too long to sing against; use Transparency mode on the AirPods instead.

Is the click in my recording?

Never. The click lives on a bus that is excluded from every recording and every export. With no headphones, switch the click to Haptic (Silent Click) so it taps through the phone and cannot reach the mic.

How do I send stems to my producer?

Record → SHARE → Tracks. Pick the parts, choose Processed or Raw, and share the bundle. Every file starts at 0:00.000 and runs the full song length, so they drop onto any session at once and line up. The bundle includes a README with the BPM, meter and sample rate. Track and stem export is part of VoxCodex Pro.

Tempo from a file name

Name your backing track like Song 110 BPM 4-4.wav and VoxCodex sets the session's tempo and meter automatically on import.

What does Pro include?

Up to 8 tracks, multi-file import, Tune, custom EQ and compressor, CLEAN depth, WAV mix, track WAVs and the aligned stem bundle. Free keeps everything you recorded playable and editable — it just exports an M4A mix and records two vocal tracks.

Restore a purchase or manage a subscription

Settings → PRO → Restore purchases brings Pro back on a new iPhone. Manage subscription opens your App Store subscriptions, where you can change plan or cancel; access continues to the end of the paid period.

Where are my songs?

In the Files app under On My iPhone → VoxCodex, as .vcsong packages. Copy them to iCloud Drive or a computer to back them up or move them.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 17 or later. VoxCodex is iPhone-only.
