For singer-songwriters and producers

Write it. Sing it.
Send it.

VoxCodex turns your iPhone into a complete vocal studio. Record to the click, stack harmonies, comp the best takes, Tune and mix — then send a clean, isolated vocal that lands on your producer's grid without being nudged.

Download on the App Store

Every take, on the grid

Phones record late. VoxCodex measures your phone's delay once, in thirty seconds, and shifts every take onto the grid.

The click is never in the file

Not in a take, not in a mix, not in a stem. Silent Click taps the beat through the phone itself, so the mic can't hear it.

Lyrics on screen

Write or paste your words, bold the hook, highlight the next line. A teleprompter for the take.

Multitrack and comping

Lead, doubles, harmonies, ad-libs — up to eight tracks. Split, nudge, duplicate, and comp the best lines from every pass.

A real mixer and chain

Noise clean, seven-band EQ, a compressor with a faceplate, Tune in the key of your song, and a Plate / Room / Cloud reverb engine.

Stems that drop in

Isolated WAV tracks, all the same length, all starting at bar one. Your producer drags them in at once and they line up.

Free and Pro

Free: record to the click, two vocal tracks plus your beat, unlimited takes and comping, lyrics, reverb, the mixer, timing calibration. Exporting is Pro — start with a 14-day free trial.

VoxCodex Pro: the file that leaves the phone — the WAV mix, track WAVs and the aligned stem bundle. $6.99 a month or $39.99 a year, each with a 14-day free trial, or $79.99 once for life.
